3. Sections, in this order: "hook" (exactly one, first), "context" (optional), "main" (one or more), "details" (optional), "impact" (optional), "conclusion" (exactly one, last). For very short videos, use only hook, main and conclusion.
4. Segment types and the wording each one requires:

   - "opinion": see TONE below.
   - "context": one short linking sentence (18 words maximum) with no number, no name and no claim. Leave "claims" empty.
